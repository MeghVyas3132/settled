01 / Why Settled exists

A wallet can say “received” before the network can protect that payment.

A payment can arrive instantly and still be unsafe.

Zero-confirmation payments are safe only when every input in the payment and its unconfirmed history can be covered by a double-spend proof. Settled traces that history, checks each input, and tells the merchant what the network can actually protect.

The blind spot

“Received” hides the risk.

A payment can show up in a wallet even when its inputs cannot be protected against a double spend.

The answer

Check the full history.

Settled follows every unconfirmed ancestor and explains which network rule determines the verdict.

02 / The zero-conf gap

Not every instant payment can be protected.

“Paid” and “safe to hand over” are different states.

Bitcoin Cash nodes can relay double-spend proofs for eligible transactions. Contract outputs and certain signature types cannot be covered by those proofs, yet ordinary payment screens do not explain the difference.
